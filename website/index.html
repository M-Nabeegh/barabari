    Questions

    Good to know.

    Is Barabari free?

    Yes. Splitting, settling, wallets and budgets are free, with no ads.

    Does Barabari send money for me?

    No. You pay however you normally do — Raast, Easypaisa, JazzCash, bank transfer or cash — and record it in Barabari so everyone’s balance stays correct.

    Do my friends need the app?

    To see the group and add expenses, yes — they join with your invite link or QR code. You can also add people by name, and they can claim their spot later.

    Does it work without internet?

    Yes. Everything saves on your phone instantly and syncs when you’re back online.
